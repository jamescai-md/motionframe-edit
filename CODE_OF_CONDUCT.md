# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be straight with people. Critique the work, never the person. Assume the other
person is trying to do good work with the information they had.

## Scope

Issues, pull requests, discussions, and any space where you are representing this
project.

## Expected

- **Critique with evidence.** "This caption placement fails at 390 px, here is the
  screenshot" is useful. "This is bad" is not.
- **Say when you are unsure.** A question is cheaper than a wrong assertion.
- **Accept that the bar is high.** This repo rejects paid dependencies and unverifiable
  claims by design. That is a design decision, not a personal attack on your patch.
- **Credit sources.** If an idea came from somewhere, name it.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or sustained disruption.
- Demanding free work, or nagging on an issue you opened.
- Presenting someone else's work as yours — including generated output passed off as
  a real capture. That is the one thing this project treats as a defect everywhere,
  and it applies to people as much as to pipelines.
- Bigotry of any kind.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, lock, or remove contributions that break this, and may block
accounts for repeated or severe behaviour. Reports go to the maintainers via a
[private advisory](https://github.com/jamescai-md/motionframe-edit/security/advisories/new)
if the matter is sensitive, or by opening an issue if it is not.
