## What it is
An agent workflow that takes a viral piece as reference and rebuilds the *shape* of it:
the beat structure, the shot pattern, the caption rhythm — not the content.

## Rules worth keeping
1. **Analyse structure, not subject matter.** What is copied is timing and sequence; the
   content must be yours or it is plagiarism with extra steps.
2. **A reference is a specification.** Write down the beat map before shooting or
   animating: durations, change points, where the hook lands.
3. **The first three seconds decide everything** — the reference's opening is the highest
   value part of the analysis.

## What it gets wrong
No licence file, and its register is growth-hacking rather than craft: it optimises for
reproduction of a winner, which is the opposite of having a point of view.

## Where this landed
`motion-director` beat-sheet format (2) and the "borrow structure, never content"
prohibition in `taste-memory` and `precedence.md` (1, 3).
