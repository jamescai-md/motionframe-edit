# taste-skill — the kind of business decides everything

**Licence:** MIT · **Source:** https://github.com/Leonxlnx/taste-skill · **Absorbed:** 2026-11-07

## What it is
Web/UI taste for coding agents. Its section 1 — *the kind of business decides everything* —
is the single most useful design idea in the whole collection, and this project's
`design-director` is built on it.

## Rules worth keeping
1. **Establish the kind of business before layout.** A dentist, a fintech and a fashion
   label are not three palettes; they are three different argument structures, densities and
   registers. Get this wrong and no amount of polish saves it.
2. **The page plan follows from the kind**: what must be answered, in what order, before a
   visitor acts.
3. **Art direction comes from reference images plus a browser check** — not from a model's
   default aesthetic. Look at the result rendered, then judge it.
4. **Name slop instead of hand-waving.** "This reads like a template" is a defect report;
   "make it pop" is not.
5. **A "never" list** is more useful than a "prefer" list, because it is checkable.

## What it gets wrong
Web only, extremely prescriptive, and in open contradiction with `ui-ux-pro-max` (gradients
banned here, load-bearing there) with no tie-breaker of its own.
