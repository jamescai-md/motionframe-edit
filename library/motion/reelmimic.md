# reelmimic — a catalogue of named visual styles

**Licence:** MIT · **Source:** https://github.com/edenfunf/reelmimic · **Absorbed:** 2026-11-07

## What it is
"Show it a video you love, get a new video in the same style." Behind that is a library of
named styles — paper cutout, anime cel, whiteboard, pixel art and dozens more — with worked
examples, and a large asset/theme tree.

## Rules worth keeping
1. **Style is a named, reproducible choice**, not a mood. If the style has a name, its
   rules can be written down and then checked.
2. **One style per piece.** Mixed styles read as indecision, not range.
3. **Examples are the specification.** A style entry without a worked frame is a wish.
4. **Derive the palette and type scale from the style**, not the reverse.

## What it gets wrong
Its volume is assets and themes rather than rules; quality is uneven across entries, and
there is no verification anywhere. Most of the 375 files are content for one lane, not
doctrine for all of them.

## Where this landed
`motion-director/references/lanes.md` documents style-as-a-menu for lane A/B, subject to
law 8 (one system per piece) · `design-director` uses (1) and (4) when deriving tokens.
Nothing from its asset tree was redistributed.
