# Basis for inclusion

`remotion` is included in this repository at the maintainer's instruction.
The maintainer states they have permission from the rights holder to
redistribute it here.

- **Upstream:** https://github.com/remotion-dev/remotion
- **Upstream licence:** Remotion licence (not free for companies)
- **Date asserted:** 2026-11-07
- **Verified by this repository:** no — no licence grant is stated in the
  upstream repository. The assertion is recorded, not independently
  evidenced. If you are the rights holder and object, open an issue and the
  folder will be removed.

The upstream licence file, where one exists, travels alongside as
`LICENCE.upstream`. That licence still governs the copied text:
this repository's own MIT licence does not extend to it.
