# The craft bar

The accept/reject test, written out. Answer every question honestly and without
credit for effort. One "no" is a fix. Four or more "no" is a rebuild.

## Story

- [ ] Can I say what this is about in one sentence, without the title?
- [ ] Is there a reason to keep watching past 3 seconds that is not "it looks nice"?
- [ ] Does the first 2 seconds contain the strongest thing in the piece?
- [ ] Does the ending close something the opening opened?
- [ ] Is the cut-away content *about* what is being said, or merely on screen with it?

## Craft

- [ ] Would a professional editor/designer put their name on this?
- [ ] Is every cut on a word boundary, padded, and free of pops?
- [ ] Are captions in output time, inside the safe zone, and never over the mouth?
- [ ] Is there any dead frame — no motion, no light, no sound — for more than a beat?
- [ ] Is the audio at target loudness, with music under the voice?
- [ ] Does it read at phone size, muted, at a glance?

## Honesty

- [ ] Is every asset that claims to be real actually the real thing?
- [ ] Is every number sourced, or absent?
- [ ] Are generated assets declared and clearly not pretending to be proof?
- [ ] Have I said what I could not check?

## Design

- [ ] One type scale, one accent with one meaning, one spacing step?
- [ ] Does the competitor-swap test fail (i.e. could this only be *this* brand)?
- [ ] Is there exactly one focal point per frame?
- [ ] Is one deviation carrying the personality, and only one?
- [ ] Does it render at 390 / 768 / 1024 / 1440 without an empty frame?

## Restraint

- [ ] Have I deleted the thing I was most proud of, and did the piece miss it?
- [ ] Are there at most two effects, and could one go?
- [ ] Is every element earning its space, or is it filling a gap?
- [ ] Would this be better 15% shorter?

## The two questions that decide

1. **Would I watch this if I did not make it?** If no, find out where attention
   drops and fix that, not the grade.
2. **If I saw this from a competitor, would I be worried?** If no, the piece is
   competent and forgettable. Competence is the floor; the deviation is the point.

## What is *not* a reason to accept

- "It took a long time."
- "The render completed."
- "All the steps were followed."
- "The user did not ask for more."
- "It matches the reference exactly." (The reference is a source, not a target.)
- "Nobody will notice." Somebody always notices the caption under the UI.
